How this was built — sources, estimates, and caveats

Scope. "Paterson causes" = nonprofits, schools, congregations, and public programs located in Paterson, NJ. Donors can be anywhere. Window: gifts dated 2020–2025 (990-PF data covers tax years 2022–2025 for most filers; earlier years come from published donor rolls).

Sources. (1) IRS Form 990-PF Part XV grant lists for every private foundation reporting a Paterson grantee (1,216 grants from 338 foundations, via Grantmakers.io/IRS e-file; each profile links to the filing). (2) Recipient-published donor honor rolls and gala sponsor lists: Paterson Habitat FY20–23 annual reports, St. Joseph's Health Foundation impact reports and 2025 gala, Oasis 2024 strategic partners, Eva's Village 2025 gala journal, NJCDC 2023 year-in-review and 2025 gala, Passaic County CASA (excluded from totals — Wayne-based). (3) Press and corporate releases, including NJ DCA Neighborhood Revitalization Tax Credit award tables FY21–FY25.

Estimated footprint. Exact amounts where published. Donor-roll tiers ("$25,000–$49,999") count at the midpoint. NRTC corporate contributions are estimated as the award total divided by the number of named contributors. Sponsorships with no published amount count at a conservative floor ($2,500–$25,000 by tier). Regional recipients are weighted for Paterson share: Catholic Charities Diocese of Paterson 50%; United Way and 4Cs of Passaic County 70%; Health Coalition and PCCC 80%; Passaic County Habitat 90%.

Confidence. High = 70%+ of the estimate is exact amounts. Medium = mostly tiers. Low = mostly NRTC shares or floors — treat as directional.

Caveats. Anonymous and pass-through gifts are excluded. Individual giving is under-counted because most Paterson nonprofits do not publish donor lists; foundations are over-represented because their gifts are public record. Duplicate reporting of the same gift by a 990 and a donor roll was removed where detectable; some remains. The "existing KTAF donor" flag only indicates a prior giving relationship with KIPP Team & Family; no internal details are included.
